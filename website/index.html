Questions.

Is Edwell free?

It's free to download, with everything open for 14 days. After that, export needs a one-time purchase. There's no subscription.

What happens after the trial?

Your projects still open, and you can keep editing and saving them. Export asks for the purchase, which opens it for good.

Which formats can I make?

16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5, from the same project. Edwell exports MP4 in H.264 or HEVC at 1080p.

Does it need the internet?

Editing and export work offline. Starting the trial and buying need a connection, because they go through the App Store.

Does it add a watermark?

No. The video you export has nothing added to it.

Can it open Premiere Rush projects?

No. Edwell can't read Rush project files. Start from your original clips, or export them from Rush while it still runs, and rebuild the edit in Edwell.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
