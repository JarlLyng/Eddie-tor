Leaving Premiere Rush on the Mac

If you used Rush to cut, title and export short videos on a Mac, Edwell does those jobs the same simple way, and you buy it once.

What happened

Adobe has retired Premiere Rush, and points Rush users to Premiere on desktop and on mobile instead.

Rush left Adobe's site and the app stores on 30 September 2025. Technical support ended on 30 September 2026, and Adobe says the apps may no longer work after that.

Adobe, Premiere Rush Discontinuation, last updated 30 September 2026, read 1 November 2026.

What carries over

The main track is magnetic, as in Rush, so clips sit back to back and the rest closes up when you cut, trim or delete. Rush's single-key shortcuts work the same way, so J, K and L shuttle and stop, S splits, Q and W trim and G adds a title. And the job is the same short loop of cut, add a title and export, in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5, as an MP4 at 1080p.

What doesn't

Rush projects don't open in Edwell. Start from your original clips, or export them from Rush while it still runs, and rebuild the edit. Edwell is a Mac app, for macOS 14 or later, so if you edited in Rush on Windows, iPhone or Android, you'll need something else there. Some of Rush's extras aren't in Edwell either, among them colour looks, animated title templates, automatic reframing and voice-over recording.

What it costs

Edwell is free for 14 days with everything open, export included. After that, one purchase opens export for good, where the full Rush was a subscription.
