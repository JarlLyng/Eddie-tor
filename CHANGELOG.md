# Changelog

All notable changes to Edwell are written down here.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- The Ambient Session panel opens where the last recording was chosen, instead of at a folder written into the code.

## [1.1] - in TestFlight

A quicker, quieter editor (#34).

### Added
- The first video or photo in a new project sets the format (16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5) to its own shape, until you pick one yourself, so a widescreen clip no longer lands cropped in a vertical frame.
- Import puts files straight on the timeline, and an empty timeline says where the first files go, with an Import button.
- Titles: four styles to start from (Big Center, Big Left, Lower Third, Caption), nine places in the frame one click each, and a short font list with every other font under All Fonts.
- A new title (G) goes where no other title shows at that moment, with its text selected, so you just type.
- Help › Keyboard Shortcuts (⌘/) is a window of its own.
- The preview shows where the playhead is and how long the video is.

### Changed
- Above the timeline there are only Split, Title, Transition and Delete, and zoom. The media bin is hidden at first and remembers how you left it.
- The timeline shows the first track of each kind and any track with a clip on it. Drag a clip past the last track to make a new one. A track's switches show when the pointer is on its name.
- Rarer settings for the project and for titles sit under More Settings.
- ⌘E opens the save panel straight away, with the file type and the in/out range below the name. The finished video has Show in Finder and Share….
- Plainer words in the inspector and the menus.

### Fixed
- Escape or a click elsewhere ends typing in a title, so Space plays again instead of adding a space.

## [1.0] - 2026-11-11

The first release on the Mac App Store: a magnetic timeline with more tracks on demand, split and ripple trims, transitions, speed, size and position, static titles with Outfit built in, volume and fades, every format from vertical to widescreen, and MP4 export in H.264 or HEVC at 1080p. Free to try for 14 days, then one purchase to keep exporting.
